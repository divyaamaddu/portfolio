YahiPe 🗺️

The digital map of your neighborhood economy. A hyperlocal marketplace that gives small shops real-time online visibility, so you explore local shops on a live map instead of a static listing. Think Google Maps + Swiggy + Justdial, built for the shop down your street.

Tap a shop to change its status, like a shop owner would.

Owners set Open, Closed, Holiday or Staff on shift, and customers see it live. Search by category, filter by open now or cheapest, and see prices up front.

  • React + Vite
  • Leaflet / Mapbox
  • Tailwind
  • Node + Express
  • MongoDB
  • Socket.io
  • OTP auth
  • SDG 8
